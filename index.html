Privacy Policy for Phone Call

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Tech Buddies (“we”, “us”, or “our”) provides Phone Call, an Android dialer and contact-management application (package name: com.techbuddies.phonecall). This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Privacy contact: techbuddies.apk@gmail.com

1. Information used on your device

With the permissions and Android roles you grant, Phone Call accesses the information needed for its calling features:

  • Contacts: names, phone numbers, contact identifiers, contact-source/account information, favorites, and available contact details or photo references. This supports contact search, caller matching, favorites, and contact management. The app can create or update contacts when you choose those actions.
  • Call history and phone information: incoming, outgoing, missed or blocked call records, numbers, cached caller details, timestamps, duration, call state, and available SIM/phone-account information. This supports recent calls, placing and managing calls, SIM selection, and call screening.
  • Blocking and spam preferences: numbers you block or mark as spam and local screening information. The current spam-detection feature uses device information and local rules; it does not submit reports to a shared online spam directory.
  • Settings and selected content: language, theme, ringtone and wallpaper selections, fake-call settings, and other preferences. Selected or downloaded media and generated ringtones may be saved on your device.

The app maintains local databases and preferences for these features. Its current implementation does not upload your address book, detailed call history, blocked-number list, or recordings to our servers. General feature-use events can still be transmitted as described below. Android or your contact-account provider may independently synchronize contacts and call information under their own settings and policies.

2. Microphone, recordings, camera, and SMS

If you start the recording feature and grant microphone access, the app captures audio and saves a recording locally. Recording availability depends on Android and your device; capturing both sides of a call is not guaranteed. Recording files may include the phone number and recording time in their filenames. You control whether to start a recording and are responsible for obtaining any permission required from participants.

Camera access is used for the camera flashlight when call-flash alerts are enabled. This feature does not take photographs or record video.

If you choose an SMS quick reply and grant the relevant permission, the app sends the selected message to the caller through your mobile provider. The recipient and provider receive the number and message; carrier charges may apply. The app does not request access to read your SMS inbox.

3. Information sent to service providers

Phone Call uses Google services for advertising, usage measurement, reliability, configuration, and feedback. These services process information outside your device.

Advertising — Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK. The SDK collects and shares IP addresses, app and ad interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account-related identifiers, including the Android advertising ID where available. IP addresses may be used to estimate general location. These data support advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention. Ads may be personalized depending on your choices, regional requirements, and service configuration. We do not supply your contacts, call records, message contents, or audio recordings to AdMob as advertising assets or targeting inputs.

Usage analytics — Google Analytics for Firebase. Analytics processes app-instance/device identifiers, device and app information, and usage events. Our events include screen views, button taps, permission results, call-feature actions, search-result counts, contact actions, export counts, and ad interactions or revenue information. Our event implementation does not attach contact names, dialed numbers, search text, message contents, or recordings to these events.

Crash and performance diagnostics — Firebase Crashlytics and Performance Monitoring. These services process installation identifiers, device and app characteristics, crash reports, stack traces, performance measurements, and related technical information to help identify failures and improve reliability. Performance information can include network-request metadata; it is not intended to include message bodies or recordings.

Configuration — Firebase Remote Config. The app retrieves settings, feature controls, and advertising configuration. Firebase may process installation identifiers and technical request information to provide this service.

Feedback — Cloud Firestore. If you submit feedback, we receive its title and description, app name and package, device model, Android version, app version, and submission time. Feedback is stored in our Firebase project and used to investigate issues and respond to product feedback. Information you choose to include in your message is also submitted. Please avoid including other people’s private information, recordings, or unnecessary sensitive details.

For more information, see Google’s Privacy Policy, Firebase privacy information, and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures.

4. Other disclosures and external services

When you place a call, send an SMS, export or share contacts, open an external communication app, or follow a link, the information necessary for that action is provided to the recipient, provider, destination, or app you select. Exported contact files can contain personal information. Other services handle received information under their own policies.

Downloading online wallpapers or other media sends a request to the content host, which can receive your IP address and technical request information.

We may disclose information we hold when required by law, to respond to valid legal requests, protect rights and security, or prevent fraud. We do not operate a service that sells address books, call histories, or recordings. Advertising-related data sharing described above remains part of the app’s operation.

5. Permissions and your choices

You can manage permissions in Android Settings → Apps → Phone Call → Permissions. You can also change your default phone app, call-screening role, notification settings, and permission to display over other apps in Android settings. Denying or revoking access can limit the related features.

Overlay access supports call-related screens over other apps. Notifications and foreground services support call alerts and ongoing call controls. Bluetooth access supports connected audio devices. Alarm, boot, vibration, and wake permissions support scheduled fake calls and alerts. These permissions do not authorize us to read the content displayed by other apps.

You can manage advertising identifiers and available advertising privacy controls through your device’s Google/Android settings. A consent message may be presented where required and configured. For questions about consent or requests to withdraw consent, contact us using the address above. Revoking a device permission or uninstalling the app does not itself erase information already processed by service providers.

6. Retention and deletion

Local caches and settings remain on your device until refreshed, removed through relevant app features, cleared through Android app-storage settings, or removed by uninstalling. Clearing app storage does not delete contacts or call records held by Android or a synchronized account. Remove those records through the relevant contact or call-history tools. Recordings, exported contact files, and other files saved outside private app storage may remain after uninstalling; delete those separately using your device’s file tools.

Feedback is retained while needed to investigate the issue, provide support, maintain relevant records, or meet legal obligations. You may request its deletion by emailing us with enough information to identify the submission, such as its approximate date and title. Do not send your address book or full call history for this purpose. We may need proportionate verification before acting on a request.

Analytics, advertising, configuration, and diagnostic information are retained according to the relevant service’s settings and retention practices. Deletion requests for information under our control can be sent to our privacy contact; provider-held data may also require use of that provider’s privacy tools. Phone Call does not currently require an app account.

7. Security and international processing

We use Android’s app-storage protections and the security features of our service providers. Google’s Mobile Ads SDK encrypts its transmitted data using TLS. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security. Protect your device and take care when sharing contact files or recordings.

Service providers may process information in countries other than your own, where privacy rules may differ. Their published privacy information explains their processing practices and applicable safeguards.

8. Privacy rights

Depending on where you live and the applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, or objection to processing, and to withdraw consent or complain to a privacy authority. Contact techbuddies.apk@gmail.com to make a request. We will assess and respond as required by applicable law, including any lawful exceptions.

9. Children’s information

We do not knowingly request children to submit personal information through feedback. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action. Parents or guardians should supervise a child’s use of calling, recording, messaging, and sharing features.

We may update this policy when the app or our practices change. We will update the date above and provide additional notice where required.
